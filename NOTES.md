- 2026-10-22: 1.0.0 build 1, first version (branch claude/trusting-ride-32v6kh, PR, not merged, no build). App #11.
  - Engine forked from Pumping (main at 3755ac7): project layout, TypeScript, expo-sqlite with a user_version migration hook, src/purchases.ts copied unchanged (with the Pro re-check: checkPro on foreground and onProChange), src/trial.ts, src/reminder.ts cut down to the one trial reminder, src/reviews.ts, the onboarding paywall sequence (value screens, "Try it free" when trial-eligible, the reminder screen, the paywall with the X after 3 seconds), the in-app paywall, PdfPreview (free SAMPLE preview), .eas/workflows deploy.yml and submit.yml, plugins/withoutPushEntitlement.js last in the plugins, CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, this log's conventions and the docs/ GitHub Pages site. Pumping itself was not touched.
  - OCR: a local Expo Module, modules/vision-ocr (Swift, expo-modules-core, autolinked from ./modules). recognize(uri, options) runs VNRecognizeTextRequest at .accurate with language correction, automaticallyDetectsLanguage (iOS 16+) and the chosen recognitionLanguages (en-US always in), on the full-resolution image with its orientation, and returns ordered lines with top-left boxes, confidence, per-word boxes (UTF-16 ranges) and the runner-up readings. renderPdf draws PDF pages to JPEGs with PDFKit/CoreGraphics (page rotation respected, locked PDFs refused). supportedLanguages lists what the phone can read. No third-party SDK, no network. Paragraph grouping in TypeScript (modules/vision-ocr/src/paragraphs.ts): reading order with same-row fixes, then breaks on a blank line's gap, going back up, a letter-height change, list markers, first-line indents, outdents and short last lines.
  - Screens: Home (Scan as the live action, Photos, Files, the free count, history with thumbnail, first line, date and page count; ghost sheet when empty), Capture (expo-camera with a page-shaped guide, auto-capture off, torch; crop and rotate with expo-image-manipulator, crop preset to the guide; reading with a highlighter sweep), Result (raw or Clean up, tap a word for its source crop, faint dotted underline on unsure words, edit per page, Copy all, Share, Export .txt/.pdf/.md, Add page), Pages tray (drag to reorder, VoiceOver move actions, delete a page), Settings (reading languages, keep originals, Pro status, restore, terms, privacy, help, email, delete every scan), onboarding and both paywalls.
  - Free tier (src/quota.ts): 3 recognitions a day (a page with no text found does not count), copy and share, documents of up to 2 pages, no export files, no Clean up. The in-app paywall opens at the daily limit, on export (free users can preview the PDF, marked SAMPLE; its Share and Print open the paywall), on Clean up, and on page 3 of a batch.
  - Pro: entitlement `pro`, offering `default`: $rc_weekly = itt_pro_weekly (3-day trial, preselected and first on the onboarding paywall), $rc_annual = itt_pro_yearly (preselected and first in-app as "Yearly, best value"). No lifetime. Prices and trial text from StoreKit; trial wording only when RevenueCat says this Apple ID is eligible.
